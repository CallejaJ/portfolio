# Prompts Nano Banana — fondos para creatividades CV-ATS

Prompts en inglés (rinde mejor). Genera cada concepto en dos formatos y guarda en `../fondos/`:

- **1:1** (1080×1080) → `concepto-N.png` (feed)
- **9:16** (1080×1920) → `concepto-N-v.png` (stories/reels)

Todos piden fondo en tu paleta (morado oscuro #150d26, acentos violeta #9333ea y cyan #22d3ee) y **sin texto**: los textos los monta `gen-cv-ads.mjs`. Deja limpio el tercio inferior (ahí van titular + CTA).

Para el vertical añade al final de cada prompt:
`vertical 9:16 format, keep the main subject in the middle third, empty space in top and bottom thirds`

---

## Concepto 1 — El robot que descarta tu CV
*El 75% de los CVs los rechaza un software antes de que los vea un humano.*

> A modern minimalist illustration of a resume document being scanned by a glowing robotic eye and a cyan laser scan beam, the document marked with a subtle red X, deep dark purple background (#150d26) with violet (#9333ea) and cyan (#22d3ee) glowing accents, clean flat design with soft gradients, dramatic rim lighting, large empty negative space in the lower third for text overlay, absolutely no letters, words or numbers anywhere, professional tech aesthetic, 1:1 square format

## Concepto 2 — Antes / después
*Mismo candidato: un CV invisible y otro que pasa el filtro.*

> Split screen comparison illustration: left side a crumpled greyish resume sinking into shadow with a faint red cross, right side a clean structured resume glowing green (#4ade80) with a checkmark rising upward, deep dark purple background (#150d26), violet and cyan glow accents, flat modern design, symmetrical composition, empty lower third for text overlay, absolutely no letters, words or numbers anywhere, 1:1 square format

## Concepto 3 — El montón
*100 candidatos por oferta; la mayoría no llega a ojos humanos.*

> A tall stack of hundreds of identical grey resume documents on a dark desk, one single resume levitating above the pile glowing with warm amber light (#fbbf24) picked by a sleek robotic arm, deep dark purple moody background with cyan rim lighting, cinematic, semi-realistic 3D render style, shallow depth of field, empty lower third for text, absolutely no letters, words or numbers anywhere, 1:1 square format

## Concepto 4 — Rechazado en segundos
*El ATS decide en segundos; el recruiter mira 6 segundos.*

> Close-up of a smartphone floating on a deep dark purple background showing an abstract blurred job application interface with a glowing red rejected cross icon, a subtle stopwatch hologram beside it, violet and cyan neon accents, modern 3D illustration style, dramatic lighting, empty lower third for text overlay, absolutely no letters, words or numbers anywhere, 1:1 square format

## Concepto 5 — Puntuación ATS
*Tu CV tiene una nota y no la conoces (espejo del "99/100" de tu marca).*

> Minimalist dashboard-style 3D illustration of a resume document next to a large circular gauge meter filled almost to the top glowing bright green (#4ade80), deep dark purple tech background (#150d26) with a subtle grid and cyan (#22d3ee) accents, clean flat UI aesthetic, soft glow, empty lower third for text overlay, absolutely no letters, words or numbers anywhere, 1:1 square format

## Concepto 6 — El embudo
*El filtro ATS visualizado: muchos entran, pocos salen.*

> Isometric 3D illustration of a large dark funnel with digital circuit texture, many grey resume documents falling into the top, only three glowing green resumes coming out of the bottom onto a desk, deep dark purple background with violet and cyan glowing accents, modern flat 3D style, soft lighting, empty lower third for text, absolutely no letters, words or numbers anywhere, 1:1 square format

## Consejos
- Genera 3–4 variaciones por prompt; elige la más limpia y con el tercio inferior más despejado.
- Mismos fondos sirven para ES y EN: el idioma va en el overlay.
- Para empezar, testa conceptos 1, 2 y 5.
